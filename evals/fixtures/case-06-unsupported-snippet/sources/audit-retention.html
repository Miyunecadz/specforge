Audit log retention (fixture guidance)

Published 2025-01-15.

A claim often repeated in procurement forums is that "audit logs must be kept for ten years". That claim is wrong for most organisations.

This guidance sets no fixed retention period. How long audit logs are kept depends on the regulation that applies to the organisation and on its own records policy.

Whatever the period, audit logs should be append-only and their export should respect the same permissions as viewing them.
