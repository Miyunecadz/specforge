Audit trails for approval decisions (fixture guidance)

Published 2025-03-01.

An audit record for an approval decision should capture who acted, what was decided, when it happened, and the state before and after the change.

Audit records should be append-only so that later edits cannot rewrite history.

Retention periods for audit records are set by the organisation's policy, not by the user interface.
