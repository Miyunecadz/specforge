Notifying people about decisions (fixture guidance)

Published 2025-03-01.

Tell the person affected by a decision what was decided, when, and what they can do next.

Email is suitable for decisions the recipient may need to keep a record of; in-app notices suit information needed only while using the product.

Whether to notify people who were not selected is a policy decision for the organisation.
